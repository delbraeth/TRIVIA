📅 This Day in History — August 13

  • 1961 — the Berlin Wall goes up. Overnight, East German troops sealed the border, tearing up streets and stringing barbed wire across 27 miles of the city. Families woke up on opposite sides. 65 years ago today. The wall stood for 28 years.
  • 1521 — Tenochtitlán falls. After a 93-day siege, Hernán Cortés captured the Aztec capital and its last emperor, Cuauhtémoc. The city became Mexico City. 505 years ago today.
  • 1889 — the pay phone is patented. William Gray of Hartford, Connecticut patented the coin-operated telephone. He'd had the idea after being unable to find a phone to call a doctor for his sick wife.

Who created Barbie, and when? — Ruth Handler, Mattel, 1959 (named for her daughter Barbara; Ken is her son)

Who patented Monopoly — and who really invented it? — Charles Darrow patented it in 1935, but it descends from Lizzie Magie's 1904 "The Landlord's Game," designed to criticize land monopolies.

Who invented corn flakes? — The Kellogg brothers, Battle Creek, Michigan (an accident with left-out boiled wheat, 1894; company founded 1906)

Who invented the dishwasher? — Josephine Cochrane, 1886 (a wealthy hostess tired of servants chipping her china)

Where did duct tape come from? — 1943 — Vesta Stoudt's idea, made by Johnson & Johnson to waterproof WWII ammo boxes. It was called "duck tape" first.

When and where was Procter & Gamble founded? — 1837, Cincinnati — candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, who were brothers-in-law and were pushed into partnership by their father-in-law.
