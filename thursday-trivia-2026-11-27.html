🕯️ Notable Deaths — Past Week

  • 🎸 Andy Taylor, 65 (Nov 23). Duran Duran's original guitarist, on "Hungry Like the Wolf," "Rio" and "A View to a Kill," and a member of the Power Station. He had prostate cancer.
  • 🎬 Gerald R. Molen, 91 (about Nov 23). Won the Best Picture Oscar for Schindler's List and produced Jurassic Park, where he has a cameo as the veterinarian. He started out as a studio truck driver.
  • 🏛️ Strobe Talbott, 80 (Nov 26). Deputy Secretary of State under Clinton and president of the Brookings Institution. Earlier, as a young man, he translated Khrushchev's memoirs.
  • 🚣 Anita DeFrantz, 74 (announced Nov 26). Won rowing bronze at the 1976 Olympics and was the first Black American and the first American woman on the IOC. In 1997 she became its first female vice president.
  • 🎸 Michael "Kidd Funkadelic" Hampton, 70 (Nov 23). Parliament-Funkadelic guitarist and Rock & Roll Hall of Famer.
  • 🎨 Mark Tildesley, 63 (Nov 24). Production designer on No Time to Die, 28 Days Later and the 2012 London Olympics opening ceremony.
  • Also: Renu Setna, 97, actor (Only Fools and Horses); Nigel Starmer-Smith, 81, rugby commentator; Catherine Clément, 87, French philosopher. Earlier this week: Veruschka, 87; Molly Meldrum, 83; Michael Giles, 84 (King Crimson).

📰 Current Events

  • 🎈 The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed to Saturday, Nov 28, at 8:30 a.m., the first weather postponement in its history. A nor'easter was forecast to bring gusts of 65–70 mph. (The parade was suspended 1942–44 during WWII.)
  • 🌧️ The Thanksgiving nor'easter cancelled more than 400 flights and knocked out power to about 375,000 customers in Virginia and Maryland. New Jersey declared a state of emergency.
  • 🐕 National Dog Show Best in Show: Rocker, a Black and Tan Coonhound. The reserve winner was Kissy, a Lhasa Apso. 2,107 dogs from 208 breeds were entered.
  • 🛍️ Adobe expects $12.9 billion in online Black Friday spending, up 9.2%. Cyber Monday should still be the biggest day, at about $15.1 billion.
  • ⚖️ A federal judge blocked the FCC's early review of ABC's broadcast licenses, siding for now with Disney in its First Amendment lawsuit.
  • 💥 The Army detonated about 5 tons of unstable lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant near Middletown. Nearby residents were advised to evacuate, and the EPA says it has detected no airborne lead so far.

🏭 Founded & Invented

Anders Celsius, born on this date in 1701, proposed his temperature scale in 1742. What was odd about his original version?
It was upside down: 0° was boiling and 100° was freezing. It was flipped after his death.

Black Friday tie-in: which company issued the first multipurpose charge card in 1950, after founder Frank McNamara forgot his wallet at a New York restaurant?
Diners Club

Sam Walton opened the first Walmart in 1962 in which Arkansas town? (Not Bentonville.)
Rogers, Arkansas

DuPont chemist Stephanie Kwolek invented which fiber in 1965? Pound for pound it's five times stronger than steel, and it's used in bulletproof vests.
Kevlar

Ruth Wakefield created the chocolate chip cookie around 1938 at her inn in Whitman, Massachusetts. What was the inn called?
The Toll House Inn. She reportedly let Nestlé use the recipe in exchange for a lifetime supply of chocolate.

🏙️ Greek immigrant Nicholas Lambrinides founded which Cincinnati chili chain in Price Hill in 1949, naming it for the view of downtown?
Skyline Chili

🧩 General Trivia

  • Animals🐕 The National Dog Show winner's breed, the Black and Tan Coonhound, was the first coonhound breed the AKC recognized, in 1945.
  • Pop Culture🥋 Bruce Lee, born today in 1940 in San Francisco's Chinatown, won Hong Kong's cha-cha championship in 1958.
  • Music🎸 Jimi Hendrix, born today in 1942, served as a paratrooper in the 101st Airborne. He was left-handed and played a right-handed Stratocaster flipped upside down.
  • Food🍽️ Legend says the Swanson TV dinner (1953) was invented to use up about 260 tons of leftover Thanksgiving turkey. The first tray was turkey, cornbread dressing, peas and sweet potatoes.
  • Food🍌 Twinkies were originally filled with banana cream. Hostess switched to vanilla when bananas were rationed in World War II.
  • Science🫐 Cranberries have air pockets inside, so ripe ones bounce and float. That's why growers flood bogs to harvest them, and why they're nicknamed "bounceberries."
  • Parade🐱 Felix the Cat was the first character balloon in the Macy's parade, in 1927.
  • Space🪐 Pluto hasn't finished a single orbit since it was discovered in 1930. One trip around the Sun takes about 248 years.
  • History🏺 Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
  • Geography🇳🇵 Nepal's flag is the only national flag that isn't a rectangle. It's two stacked pennants.
  • Invention🫧 Bubble Wrap was invented in 1957 as textured wallpaper. That flopped, and it found its real job protecting IBM computers in shipping.
