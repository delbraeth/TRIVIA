🏭 Founded & Invented

Who invented the Slinky, and how? — Richard James, 1943 — a Navy engineer working on ship stabilizer springs knocked one off a shelf and watched it "walk." Sold at Gimbels in 1945.

When was Amazon founded? — 1994, Jeff Bezos — in a Bellevue, WA garage. His first choice of name was "Cadabra."

What does WD-40 stand for? — "Water Displacement, 40th formula" — Rocket Chemical Co., 1953. The first 39 attempts didn't work.

Who invented the Super Soaker? — Lonnie Johnson — a NASA/JPL engineer who stumbled on it in 1982 while tinkering with a heat pump. Launched 1990.

Who invented the Band-Aid? — Earle Dickson, 1920 — a Johnson & Johnson cotton buyer whose new wife kept cutting herself in the kitchen.
