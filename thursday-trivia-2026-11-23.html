🌍 Capitals

🇲🇾 What's the official capital of Malaysia? (Bonus: where does the government actually work?)
Kuala Lumpur. Most government offices moved to the planned city of Putrajaya around 1999.

🇫🇴 What's the capital of the Faroe Islands?
Tórshavn. Its parliament has met on the Tinganes peninsula since Viking times.

🇵🇾 What's the capital of Paraguay, one of South America's two landlocked countries?
Asunción. The other landlocked country is Bolivia.

🇪🇹 Which capital's name means "new flower," and is the African Union's headquarters?
Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

🏭 Founded & Invented

The first jukebox debuted on this date in 1889. How did customers listen, and what did it cost?
Through four listening tubes, for a nickel a play. It was put in by Louis Glass of the Pacific Phonograph Co.

In 1953, which company turned about 260 tons of leftover Thanksgiving turkey into the first big-selling "TV dinner"?
Swanson. It sold for 98 cents.

Which turkey brand has run a holiday "Turkey Talk-Line" for nervous cooks since 1981?
Butterball

Three cranberry growers formed which cooperative in 1930? It's still owned by its farmers.
Ocean Spray

Ruth Siems, a home economist at General Foods, invented which 1972 Thanksgiving shortcut?
Stove Top Stuffing

For National Espresso Day: in which Italian city did Angelo Moriondo patent the first espresso machine, in 1884?
Turin. It brewed coffee in bulk, not one cup at a time.

🧩 General Trivia

  • Animals🦛 Hippos make a reddish oil sometimes called "blood sweat." It isn't blood or sweat. It works as a natural sunscreen and may fight germs.
  • TVTARDIS stands for "Time And Relative Dimension In Space." It looks like a 1960s London police box.
  • History🦃 Ben Franklin never formally proposed the turkey as the national bird. In a 1784 letter to his daughter, he called it "a much more respectable bird" than the bald eagle.
  • HistoryIn 1939, FDR moved Thanksgiving up a week to give stores a longer shopping season. Critics called it "Franksgiving." Congress fixed it to the fourth Thursday in 1941.
  • WordsThe bird got its name by mistake. Europeans first used "turkey" for the guinea fowl, which came to them through the Ottoman Empire (Turkey). The name stuck to the American bird.
  • Sports🏈 The Dallas Cowboys have hosted a Thanksgiving game almost every year since 1966. The only exceptions were 1975 and 1977. The Lions host the other traditional game.
  • Words☕ "Espresso" comes from the Italian for "pressed out." Hot water is forced through tightly packed coffee grounds.
  • Words"Jukebox" comes from the "juke joint." "Juke" is a Gullah word meaning rowdy or disorderly.
  • MathIn the Fibonacci sequence, each number is the sum of the two before it. Fibonacci introduced it to Europe in 1202 with a puzzle about breeding rabbits.
  • Tennis🎾 Carlos Alcaraz and Jannik Sinner both won six titles in 2026, tied for the most on tour.
