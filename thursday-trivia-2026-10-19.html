📜 This Day in History — October 19

  • 1781 — Cornwallis surrenders at Yorktown, 245 years ago today. Cornwallis said he was sick, so Gen. Charles O'Hara tried to hand the sword to the French general Rochambeau. He was sent to Washington, who had his own second, Benjamin Lincoln, accept it.
  • 1987 — Black Monday. The Dow fell 508 points, or 22.6%. That's still the biggest one-day percentage drop in its history.
  • 1864 — The St. Albans Raid. About 20 Confederates crossed from Canada, robbed three banks in St. Albans, Vermont, and fled back over the border. It was the northernmost land action of the Civil War. On the same day, Sheridan's ride turned defeat into victory at Cedar Creek, Virginia.
  • Also: Ferdinand of Aragon and Isabella of Castile marry in Valladolid (1469).

🧩 General Trivia

  • KentuckyKentucky is officially a commonwealth, one of four states with that title, with Massachusetts, Pennsylvania and Virginia.
  • HistoryThe Civil War's two presidents, Abraham Lincoln and Jefferson Davis, were both born in Kentucky, less than a year and roughly 100 miles apart.
  • ScienceTonic water glows blue-white under a black light, because the quinine in it is fluorescent.
  • HistoryLegend says the British band at Yorktown played "The World Turned Upside Down." The phrase became the title of a song in Hamilton.
  • RacingRachel Alexandra, daughter of Medaglia d'Oro, won the 2009 Preakness. She was the first filly to win it since 1924.
  • College FBAuburn–Georgia, which Auburn won Saturday over No. 1 UGA, is called the "Deep South's Oldest Rivalry." It was first played in 1892.
  • ScienceFor Nuclear Science Week: the first self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction (Chicago Pile-1, Dec 2, 1942) took place under the stands of the University of Chicago's Stagg Field.
  • AnimalsFor Wolf Awareness Week: gray wolves were brought back to Yellowstone in 1995. They thinned the elk herds that had overgrazed the riverbanks, which let the willows grow back.
  • TennisDavidovich Fokina lost his first five tour-level finals. He has now won three straight, including Shanghai, and is on a 10-match win streak in China.
  • FinanceBlack Monday led the NYSE to adopt "circuit breakers", which halt trading during a sharp drop.
