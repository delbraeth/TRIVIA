📜 This Day in History — November 26

  • 1789 — The first national Thanksgiving under the Constitution. George Washington proclaimed Thursday, Nov 26, 1789, a day of "public thanksgiving and prayer."
  • 1942 — Casablanca premiered at New York's Hollywood Theatre, rushed out weeks after the Allies landed in North Africa and captured the real Casablanca.
  • 1965 — France launched Astérix-1 from Hammaguir, Algeria, becoming the third country, after the USSR and the U.S., to put a satellite in orbit with its own rocket.
  • 2018 — NASA's InSight lander touched down on Mars on the flat plain Elysium Planitia, to study marsquakes and the planet's interior.
  • Born today: Eugène Ionesco (1909), Charles Schulz (1922), Robert Goulet (1933), Rich Little (1938), Tina Turner (1939).

🧩 General Trivia

  • Animals🦃 The fleshy flap that hangs over a turkey's beak is the snood; the red skin under its chin is the wattle. Only males (toms) gobble.
  • Animals🦃 Wild turkeys can fly, in short bursts up to about 55 mph, and they sleep in trees. Farm-raised broad-breasted turkeys are too heavy to fly.
  • Food🥧 Minnesota raises more turkeys than any other state, about 40 million a year.
  • Science😴 Turkey doesn't have much more tryptophan than chicken. The post-dinner sleepiness comes mostly from eating a huge, carb-heavy meal.
  • History🌽 Squanto (Tisquantum) could speak English with the Pilgrims because he had been kidnapped in 1614 and taken to Spain and England.
  • History🦌 At the 1621 harvest feast, Massasoit arrived with about 90 Wampanoag men, more than the 50 or so colonists. They brought five deer, and the celebration lasted three days.
  • History🪨 Plymouth Rock split in two in 1774 when townspeople tried to move it. The "1620" carved on it was added in 1880.
  • Music🔔 "Jingle Bells" (James Lord Pierpont, 1857) was the first song played in space. Gemini 6 astronauts Wally Schirra and Tom Stafford played it on a harmonica and bells in December 1965.
  • TV🐕 The National Dog Show is put on by the Kennel Club of Philadelphia, which dates to 1879. NBC has aired it after the Macy's parade since 2002.
  • Music🎤 Tina Turner, born on this date in 1939, was born Anna Mae Bullock. Her small Tennessee hometown inspired her 1973 hit "Nutbush City Limits."
