📜 This Day in History — November 11

  • 1620 — Aboard the ship off Cape Cod, 41 male passengers signed the Mayflower Compact. The date is Nov 11 on the Old Style calendar they used.
  • 1889 — Washington became the 42nd state. It is the only state named for a president.
  • 1918 — The Armistice ended the fighting in World War I at the "eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month." It was signed in a railway carriage in the Forest of Compiègne around 5 a.m. and took effect at 11 a.m. Today is Veterans Day in the U.S. and Remembrance Day in the Commonwealth.
  • 1921 — President Harding dedicated the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier at Arlington National Cemetery.
  • 1975 — Australia's Governor-General dismissed Prime Minister Gough Whitlam, the only time that has happened. Angola became independent from Portugal the same day.
  • Born today: Fyodor Dostoevsky (1821), George S. Patton (1885), Kurt Vonnegut (1922), Jonathan Winters (1925), Carlos Fuentes (1928), Stanley Tucci (1960), Demi Moore (1962), Calista Flockhart (1964), Leonardo DiCaprio (1974).

🧩 General Trivia

  • HistoryThe Armistice was signed about six hours before it took effect, and the fighting continued until 11 a.m. About 2,700 soldiers died on the last day. American Henry Gunther was killed at 10:59 a.m.
  • HistoryIn 1940, Hitler made France sign its surrender in the same railway carriage, at the same spot in Compiègne.
  • GrammarThe holiday is officially spelled "Veterans Day", with no apostrophe. It was renamed from Armistice Day in 1954.
  • SciencePoppies did so well on Western Front battlefields because shelling churned up the soil, and rubble added lime to it. Poppy seeds sprout when soil is disturbed.
  • Pop cultureSingles' Day is said to have started in 1993 with students at Nanjing University, who chose 11/11 because the date is all ones.
  • Local🔴 The Boones had four big leaguers. Bob managed the Reds, and both of his sons played for Cincinnati: Bret (1994–98) and Aaron (1997–2003).
  • BaseballSal Stewart is the 14th rookie since 1920 with at least 30 HR and 100 RBI. The Reds drafted him 32nd overall in 2022.
  • FoodOne story says "sundae" is spelled that way so the name wouldn't seem to disrespect the Sabbath. The dessert was supposedly sold on Sundays when ice-cream sodas weren't allowed.
  • FootballKirk Cousins is going back to the Vikings, where he was the starting QB from 2018 to 2023.
  • HockeyCarolina–Seattle in Helsinki is part of the NHL Global Series. Helsinki also hosted the first NHL regular-season games in Finland, in 2017.
