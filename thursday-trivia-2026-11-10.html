📜 This Day in History — November 10

  • 1871 — Henry Morton Stanley found Dr. David Livingstone at Ujiji, on Lake Tanganyika (this is the traditional date). Supposedly he greeted him with "Dr. Livingstone, I presume?"
  • 1938 — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, founder of modern Turkey, died at Istanbul's Dolmabahçe Palace at 9:05 a.m.
  • 1969 — Sesame Street premiered. It aired first on NET, the predecessor of PBS.
  • 1975 — The freighter SS Edmund Fitzgerald sank in a storm on Lake Superior, and all 29 crew members died.

🧩 General Trivia

  • HistoryEvery Nov 10 at 9:05 a.m., Turkey stops for a minute of silence, with sirens and traffic halted, to mark the moment Atatürk died. Clocks at Dolmabahçe Palace are kept at 9:05.
  • MusicGordon Lightfoot's "The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald" reached No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1976, a year after the sinking.
  • MythNo one can confirm Stanley said "Dr. Livingstone, I presume?" The pages for those days are missing from his diary, and Livingstone never mentioned the line.
  • GemsIn 1958, Harry Winston sent the Hope Diamond to the Smithsonian by registered first-class mail, in a plain brown paper package.
  • TVSesame Street first aired on NET, National Educational Television. PBS didn't start until 1970.
  • MarinesAt every Marine Corps birthday ball, the first slice of cake goes to the oldest Marine there, who hands it to the youngest.
  • NamesMartin Luther was named for St. Martin of Tours because he was baptized on Nov 11, St. Martin's feast day.
  • FilmRichard Burton got seven Oscar nominations for acting and never won. He married Elizabeth Taylor twice.
  • GeographyBig Bend National Park follows the Rio Grande for 118 miles along the Mexican border, and the river's big curve there gives the park its name.
  • NBAGolden State beat New Orleans 138–87 on Monday. A 51-point win is rare in the NBA. The record is 73 points, set by Memphis over Oklahoma City in 2021.
  • BaseballThe Reds' Rookies of the Year: Frank Robinson, Pete Rose, Tommy Helms, Johnny Bench, Pat Zachry, Chris Sabo, Scott Williamson, Jonathan India, and now Sal Stewart.
