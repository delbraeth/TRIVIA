📍 Capitals Quiz

Capital of Slovakia? Bratislava — the only national capital that borders two other countries (Austria and Hungary).

Capital of Kenya? Nairobi — the only capital city in the world with a national park inside its limits.

Capital of Oman? Muscat — a natural harbor on the Gulf of Oman, ringed by the Al Hajar mountains.

Capital of Panama? Panama City — founded 1519, sacked by Henry Morgan in 1671; the ruins of "Panamá Viejo" are a UNESCO site.

🎨 Arts & Literature

Who painted The Last Supper on a Milan monastery wall? Leonardo da Vinci — he used experimental tempera on dry plaster, which is why it began flaking within his lifetime.

Who wrote Fahrenheit 451? Ray Bradbury — typed on a rented basement typewriter at UCLA for ten cents per half hour.

Which composer wrote Peter and the Wolf, giving each character its own instrument? Sergei Prokofiev — 1936; the cat is a clarinet, the duck an oboe, the wolf three horns.

"Look on my Works, ye Mighty, and despair!" — which poet wrote "Ozymandias"? Percy Bysshe Shelley — 1818, written as a friendly contest with a friend.

Who wrote Lolita and Pale Fire? Vladimir Nabokov — a Russian émigré who also published serious work on butterflies.

Which artist made the Campbell's Soup Cans? Andy Warhol — 32 canvases, one for each variety Campbell's sold in 1962.

🏭 Founded & Invented

Who invented Liquid Paper / correction fluid? Bette Nesmith Graham, 1956 — a Dallas bank secretary mixing paint in her kitchen; her son Michael became a Monkee.

Who accidentally discovered vulcanized rubber? Charles Goodyear, 1839 — dropped a rubber-sulfur mix on a hot stove. He died in debt; Goodyear Tire was named for him in 1898, 38 years later.

Who invented the windshield wiper? Mary Anderson, patented 1903 — inspired by watching a streetcar driver lean out into sleet. Her patent expired before cars went mainstream.

When was Twitter founded, and what was the first tweet? 2006 — Jack Dorsey: "just setting up my twttr"

Who designed the Pringles can? Fredric Baur, P&G Cincinnati, 1966 — he was so proud of it that his ashes were buried in one in 2008.

Where and when was Wendy's founded? Columbus, Ohio, 1969 — Dave Thomas — named for his daughter Melinda, nicknamed "Wendy."

🧩 General Trivia

  • ScienceChili heat comes from capsaicin, which birds can't taste — the plant evolved it to stop mammals from chewing the seeds and let birds spread them instead.
  • SpaceJupiter has at least 95 confirmed moons, and Ganymede is larger than the planet Mercury.
  • GeographyThe Caspian Sea is the largest lake on Earth — bigger than the entire country of Japan.
  • AnimalsCrows recognize individual human faces, hold grudges for years, and pass the grudge to their offspring.
  • BodyThe smallest bone in your body is the stapes in the middle ear — about 3 millimeters long.
  • FoodAlmonds aren't nuts — they're the seed inside a fruit closely related to the peach.
  • Language"Sandwich" is named for John Montagu, the 4th Earl of Sandwich, who wanted food he could eat without leaving the gaming table.
  • HistoryThe fax machine was patented in 1843 by Alexander Bain — 33 years before the telephone.
  • Sports"Hat trick" comes from cricket: a bowler who took three wickets on three consecutive balls was awarded a hat.
  • Pop CultureOscar the Grouch was orange for the whole first season of Sesame Street; Jim Henson turned him green for season two.
  • Weather"Watermelon snow" is real — pink alpine snowfields get their color from a red-pigmented algae, Chlamydomonas nivalis.
  • LocalCincinnati's Art Deco Carew Tower (1930) was an early "city within a city" — hotel, shops and offices in one tower — and stayed the tallest building in town for about 80 years.
  • Reds bar-betJoe Nuxhall pitched for the Reds in 1944 at 15 years, 10 months — still the youngest player in modern MLB history. He later spent 37 years in the Reds broadcast booth.
