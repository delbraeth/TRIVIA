📜 This Day in History — November 25

  • 1863 — At the Battle of Missionary Ridge, Ulysses S. Grant's Union troops broke the Confederate siege of Chattanooga. Soldiers charged up the ridge without orders.
  • 1915 — Albert Einstein presented the field equations of general relativity to the Prussian Academy of Sciences in Berlin.
  • 1963 — JFK's state funeral and burial at Arlington. It was John F. Kennedy Jr.'s third birthday, and he saluted his father's casket.

🏭 Founded & Invented

Karl Benz was born on this date in 1844. What did he patent in January 1886, often called the first practical gasoline car?
The Benz Patent-Motorwagen. In 1888 his wife Bertha drove it about 66 miles to her mother's, buying fuel at a pharmacy on the way.

🧩 General Trivia

  • History⚓ On Nov 25, 1120, the White Ship sank off Barfleur, Normandy, drowning William Adelin, the only legitimate son of England's Henry I. The fight over who would succeed Henry led to the civil war known as "the Anarchy."
  • Baseball⚾ Joe DiMaggio, born on this date in 1914, hit in 56 straight games in 1941. After the streak ended, he hit in 16 more in a row.
  • Food🦃 A turkey's wishbone is its furcula: two collarbones fused together. It braces the bird's chest when it flaps its wings.
  • History📚 Andrew Carnegie, born Nov 25, 1835, paid for 2,509 libraries around the world, 1,689 of them in the U.S.
  • Words🛍️ "Black Friday" was spread by Philadelphia police in the 1950s and '60s to describe the traffic mess the day after Thanksgiving. The "stores go into the black" explanation came later.
  • Parade🎈 Snoopy has been a Macy's parade balloon more times than any other character.
  • Parade🎈 The Macy's parade was canceled from 1942 to 1944. The rubber and helium were needed for World War II, and the balloons were donated to the war effort.
  • Geography🇨🇦 Canada has more lakes than the rest of the world combined, by most counts more than half of all the lakes on Earth.
  • Music🎄 On Band Aid's single, Bono sang the line "Well tonight thank God it's them instead of you." He reportedly didn't want to, because of how it sounded.
