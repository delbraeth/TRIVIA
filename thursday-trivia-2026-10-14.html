📺 Classic TV — Actors & Roles

Born 99 years ago today: which future James Bond played gentleman thief Simon Templar on The Saint (1962–69)?
Roger Moore

On Irwin Allen's The Time Tunnel (1966–67), who played scientist Dr. Tony Newman? (Hard)
James Darren, a.k.a. Moondoggie in Gidget. Robert Colbert played Doug Phillips.

Who played the English butler on Mr. Belvedere (1985–90)?
Christopher Hewett. The dad, sportswriter George Owens, was played by Brewers broadcaster Bob Uecker.

On Silver Spoons (1982–87), whose character Ricky Stratton lived in a mansion with a ride-on model train?
Ricky Schroder (Joel Higgins played his dad, Edward)

Who played retired Judge Milton C. Hardcastle on Hardcastle and McCormick (1983–86)?
Brian Keith (Daniel Hugh Kelly played Mark "Skid" McCormick)

On Life Goes On (1989–93), who played Corky Thatcher, one of the first lead characters with Down syndrome on network TV?
Chris Burke. Patti LuPone played his mother; the theme was the Beatles' "Ob-La-Di, Ob-La-Da."

🎨 Arts & Literature

Published 100 years ago today, which book's title bear was based on a toy that the author's son first called "Edward"?
Winnie-the-Pooh by A. A. Milne, illustrated by E. H. Shepard. "Winnie" came from Winnipeg, a black bear at the London Zoo.

Which famous medieval "tapestry," about 230 feet long, tells the story of the Battle of Hastings, and what is it technically?
The Bayeux Tapestry. It's an embroidery, wool stitched on linen. Halley's Comet appears in it.

Which Tom Wolfe book (1979) opens its astronaut story with Chuck Yeager breaking the sound barrier?
The Right Stuff. In the 1983 film, playwright Sam Shepard played Yeager.

Born Oct 14, 1894, which poet is famous for lowercase letters and wrote "i carry your heart with me(i carry it in"?
E. E. Cummings

Which philosopher, born Oct 14, 1906, coined "the banality of evil" in her book on the Adolf Eichmann trial? (Hard)
Hannah Arendt (Eichmann in Jerusalem, 1963)

Which Czech composer's cycle Má vlast includes the river portrait "Vltava," also called "The Moldau"?
Bedřich Smetana

🏭 Founded & Invented

Ralph Lauren, born today in 1939, started his Polo brand in 1967 selling what?
Wide neckties

After a 1902 cartoon of Theodore Roosevelt sparing a bear, which Brooklyn shopkeeper made a stuffed "Teddy's bear"?
Morris Michtom, with his wife Rose. He went on to found the Ideal Toy Company.

Which dessert was named at New York's Delmonico's in 1867 to mark the U.S. purchase of a territory?
Baked Alaska, credited to chef Charles Ranhofer

Which president, born today in 1890, signed the 1956 law creating the Interstate Highway System?
Dwight D. Eisenhower. The system is officially named for him.

World Standards Day: the ISO was founded in Geneva in 1947. Why is it "ISO" and not "IOS"?
It comes from the Greek isos, "equal," so the short name is the same in every language.

Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos in 2003 at age 19. Which two words did she blend to name it?
"Therapy" and "diagnosis"

🧠 General Trivia

  • HistoryThe Battle of Hastings wasn't at Hastings. It was fought about 7 miles away, at a place now called Battle, East Sussex. William was crowned at Westminster Abbey on Christmas Day 1066.
  • AviationChuck Yeager flew with two broken ribs from a horseback fall. Engineer Jack Ridley gave him a sawed-off broom handle to close the X-1's hatch.
  • HistoryTheodore Roosevelt survived his 1912 shooting because the bullet went through a 50-page folded speech and a steel glasses case. He spoke for over an hour: "It takes more than that to kill a Bull Moose."
  • HistoryAt 35, Martin Luther King Jr. was the youngest Nobel Peace Prize winner up to then. He gave the $54,123 prize money to the civil rights movement.
  • PresidentsEisenhower was born David Dwight Eisenhower in Denison, Texas. His mother switched the names around so he wouldn't be confused with his father, David.
  • TennisDjokovic's 102 titles put him one behind Roger Federer (103). The Open Era record is Jimmy Connors' 109.
  • MoviesIn the 1994 Street Fighter, Raúl Juliá played M. Bison partly because his kids loved the game. He died two months before it came out.
  • ScienceThe sperm whale (see Whalefall) has the largest brain of any animal ever, about 17 pounds.
  • FoodNational Dessert Day: tiramisu means "pick me up" in Italian, from the espresso and cocoa.
  • MusicUsher, born today in 1978, headlined the 2024 Super Bowl halftime show in Las Vegas and got married the same night.
  • NHLThe Tkachuks' father, Keith Tkachuk, scored 538 NHL goals. Brady and Matthew played together for the U.S. at the 2025 4 Nations Face-Off.
