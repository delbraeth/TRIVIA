📜 This Day in History — November 3

  • 1948 — The Chicago Daily Tribune's early edition was dated today with the headline "DEWEY DEFEATS TRUMAN." Truman won, and a day later he held up a copy for photographers at St. Louis Union Station.
  • 1957 — The Soviet Union launched Sputnik 2 with Laika, a stray dog from the streets of Moscow, the first animal to orbit Earth. She died within hours from overheating, which wasn't admitted until 2002.
  • 2016 — Just after midnight in Cleveland, the Cubs won Game 7, 8–7 in 10 innings, their first World Series title in 108 years. A 17-minute rain delay came before the 10th inning.

LBJ won 61% of the vote, and D.C. residents voted for president for the first time (1964). Nixon's "silent majority" speech (1969). Bill Clinton was elected (1992). 🧩 General Trivia

  • CivicsThe secret ballot is often called the "Australian ballot" because it was first used in Victoria and South Australia in 1856. 🟦 Louisville, Ky. was the first U.S. city to adopt it, in 1888.
  • Words"Candidate" comes from the Latin candidatus, "dressed in white." Romans running for office wore togas whitened with chalk.
  • WordsA "bellwether" was the castrated ram (a wether) that wore a bell and led the flock. 🟥 Ohio voted for the winning presidential candidate in every election from 1964 through 2016.
  • SportsThe NBA left tonight open on purpose, as it did on Election Day in 2022 and 2024, to encourage players and fans to vote.
  • SpaceMariner 10, launched Nov 3, 1973, was the first spacecraft to visit Mercury and the first to use a gravity assist, swinging past Venus in 1974.
  • JapanJapan's Culture Day is on Nov 3 because it was Emperor Meiji's birthday. Japan's postwar constitution was also proclaimed on this date in 1946.
  • FoodOn National Sandwich Day: one story says the hoagie is named for Philadelphia's World War I Hog Island shipyard, whose workers were "Hog Islanders." It's Philadelphia's official sandwich.
  • BuildingsOne World Trade Center opened on Nov 3, 2014. It's exactly 1,776 feet tall, for the year of the Declaration of Independence.
  • PoliticsMichael Dukakis (born today, 1933) was running for president when his cousin Olympia Dukakis won the 1988 Oscar for Moonstruck.
  • MoneyPanama, which split from Colombia on this date in 1903, has no paper money of its own. The balboa is tied 1:1 to the U.S. dollar, and U.S. bills are what people actually spend.
