📜 This Day in History — October 22

  • 1797 — André-Jacques Garnerin made the first parachute jump without a rigid frame. He cut loose from a hydrogen balloon about 3,000 feet over Paris's Parc Monceau and swung so hard on the way down that he got sick, but he landed safely.
  • 1836 — Sam Houston was sworn in as the first elected president of the Republic of Texas, six months after winning at San Jacinto.
  • 1883 — The Metropolitan Opera opened on Broadway at 39th Street with Gounod's Faust.
  • Also: the FBI killed bank robber "Pretty Boy" Floyd in a field near East Liverpool, Ohio (1934). JFK went on TV to announce a naval "quarantine" of Cuba (1962). Jean-Paul Sartre turned down the Nobel Prize in Literature (1964).

  • Chiricahua became America's 64th national park. Trump signed the bill Oct 20 for the Arizona "Wonderland of Rocks," a former national monument of more than 12,000 acres of rhyolite spires about 130 miles east of Tucson. It's the first new national park since 2020.

  • Russia freed Stephen Hubbard, a 74-year-old retired American teacher held since 2022, during envoy Steve Witkoff's visit. The Kremlin says Putin is ready to resume three-way talks with Ukraine and the U.S. in November.

🧩 General Trivia

  • GeographyWith Chiricahua now the 64th, California still has the most national parks: 9. Alaska is second with 8.
  • NatureThe Chiricahuas are a "sky island," a forested mountain range rising out of the desert. The Chiricahua Apache called the area the "Land of Standing-Up Rocks."
  • AnimalsA dog's nose print is unique, like a human fingerprint, and has been used to identify dogs.
  • AnimalsThe basenji, an African hunting breed, doesn't bark. It makes a yodel-like sound called a "baroo."
  • ScienceThe "Brazil nut effect": shake a can of mixed nuts and the biggest ones rise to the top. Physicists call it granular convection.
  • WordsThe fruit came first: the color orange is named after it. Before then, English speakers called the color geoluhread, or "yellow-red."
  • HistoryIn 2000, skydiver Adrian Nicholas safely jumped with a parachute built to Leonardo da Vinci's c. 1485 pyramid design, made of canvas and wood.
  • HistorySam Houston is the only person elected governor of two different states, Tennessee and Texas.
  • FoodChicago's tavern-style pizza has a thin, cracker-crisp crust and is "party cut" into squares, so bar crowds could share it.
  • SportsThe U.S. Open Cup, first played in 1914, is the oldest ongoing national soccer competition in the U.S.
  • SportsBrewers starter Jacob Misiorowski threw a 104.8 mph pitch in this year's NLDS, the fastest postseason pitch since tracking began in 2008.
