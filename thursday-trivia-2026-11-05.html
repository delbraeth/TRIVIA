📜 This Day in History — November 5

  • 1605 — Guy Fawkes was caught guarding 36 barrels of gunpowder in a cellar under the House of Lords, foiling the Gunpowder Plot to blow up King James I. Britain still marks the day as Bonfire Night.
  • 1872 — Susan B. Anthony voted in the presidential election in Rochester, N.Y. She was arrested two weeks later, convicted and fined $100, which she never paid.
  • 1940 — FDR won an unprecedented third term, beating Wendell Willkie. He is the only president elected more than twice.
  • 1994 — George Foreman, 45, knocked out Michael Moorer in the 10th round and became the oldest heavyweight champion, 20 years after losing the title to Ali.
  • Also: George Selden got a U.S. patent for the automobile (1895). Parker Brothers released Monopoly (1935). Nixon was elected (1968). In Back to the Future, Nov 5, 1955 is the day Doc Brown dreamed up the flux capacitor. 🧩 General Trivia

  • WordsThe word "guy" comes from Guy Fawkes. Ragged Fawkes effigies burned on bonfires were called "guys," and the word came to mean any man.
  • UKBefore every State Opening of Parliament, the Yeomen of the Guard search the Westminster cellars, a ceremony that goes back to the Gunpowder Plot.
  • MoviesIn Back to the Future, Doc Brown dials Nov 5, 1955 into the DeLorean. It's the day he hit his head on his sink and pictured the flux capacitor.
  • BeerGuinness looks black, but it's actually a very dark ruby red. Hold a glass up to a light to see it.
  • ScienceOnly about 1–2% of people have naturally red hair. Scotland has the highest share of any country.
  • BoxingForeman beat Moorer in 1994 wearing the same red trunks he wore when he lost to Ali in the 1974 "Rumble in the Jungle."
  • F1Alan Jones's father, Stan Jones, won the 1959 Australian Grand Prix.
  • ReligionSir Lloyd Geering, who died at 108, was tried for heresy by New Zealand's Presbyterian Church in 1967. The charges were dismissed.
  • PoliticsMississippi's Senate race came down to 43 votes with about 15,000 ballots still to count.
  • TennisThe WTA Finals were moved from Riyadh to Indian Wells in July because of safety concerns over the Iran war. Saudi Arabia's three-year hosting contract ended six months early.
