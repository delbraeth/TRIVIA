Which Mexican painter made a career of unflinching self-portraits after a streetcar accident at 18? — Frida Kahlo

Which American painter is known for giant close-up flowers and New Mexico desert skulls? — Georgia O'Keeffe

🏭 Founded & Invented

Who invented the Segway, and when? — Dean Kamen, unveiled 2001 — hyped for a year under the code name "Ginger" as a device that would reshape cities

What does NERF stand for? — Non-Expanding Recreational Foam — the first Nerf ball came from Parker Brothers in 1969, out of a game prototype by Reyn Guyer

Who created Jenga, and where does the name come from? — Leslie Scott, 1983 — from the Swahili kujenga, "to build"; she grew up playing a version of it in Ghana

Who invented Hot Wheels? — Elliot Handler, Mattel, 1968 — his wife Ruth had invented Barbie nine years earlier

Where and when was Tabasco sauce created? — Avery Island, Louisiana, 1868 — Edmund McIlhenny; still aged three years in oak barrels on the same salt dome
