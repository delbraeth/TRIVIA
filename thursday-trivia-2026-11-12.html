📜 This Day in History — November 12

  • 1912 — A search party found the tent holding the frozen bodies of Robert Falcon Scott and two companions in Antarctica. They had died in March, about 11 miles from a supply depot, on the way back from the South Pole.
  • 1918 — The day after the Armistice, Austria declared itself a republic, ending more than 600 years of Habsburg rule.
  • 1954 — Ellis Island closed. About 12 million immigrants passed through it from 1892 on. The last person released was a Norwegian sailor, Arne Petersen.
  • 1981 — Columbia (STS-2) launched, and it became the first crewed spacecraft to go to space twice.
  • 2014 — The European Space Agency's Philae made the first landing on a comet, 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko. Its harpoons didn't fire, so it bounced twice before stopping.
  • Born today: Elizabeth Cady Stanton (1815), Sun Yat-sen (1866), Grace Kelly (1929), Michael Ende (1929), Wallace Shawn (1943), Neil Young (1945), Megan Mullally (1958), Nadia Comăneci (1961), Sammy Sosa (1968), Ryan Gosling (1980), Anne Hathaway (1982).

🧩 General Trivia

  • SportsAt the 1976 Montreal Olympics, Nadia Comăneci (born today in 1961) scored the first perfect 10 in gymnastics. The scoreboard showed "1.00" because it could only display three digits.
  • BaseballSammy Sosa (born today in 1968) is the only player with three 60-homer seasons: 66 in 1998, 63 in 1999 and 64 in 2001. He didn't lead the league in any of those years.
  • BaseballMisiorowski's fastball averaged 100.6 mph. In June he threw a 105.5 mph pitch, the fastest by a starting pitcher in the Statcast era.
  • FootballDick Hoak is the only assistant coach who worked under both Chuck Noll and Bill Cowher.
  • Football🐅 The Bengals first wore the all-white "White Bengal" uniforms against Miami in 2022. They're 5–1 in white helmets.
  • DrinksThe phrase "happy hour" comes from the U.S. Navy in the 1910s, when it meant scheduled entertainment on board ship, such as boxing matches and movies. It came to mean drinking during Prohibition.
  • WeatherA nor'easter is named for its strong winds from the northeast, which blow in off the Atlantic as the storm moves up the coast.
  • HistoryGrace Kelly (born today in 1929) won the Best Actress Oscar for The Country Girl in 1955 and married Prince Rainier III of Monaco the next year, which ended her film career at age 26.
  • SpacePhilae lost power about 60 hours after landing, because it had come to rest in a shadow where its solar panels got little sun. It briefly woke up again in June 2015.
  • PoliticsMississippi's last Democratic senator was John Stennis, who served from 1947 to 1989.
