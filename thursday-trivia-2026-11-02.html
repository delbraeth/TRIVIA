📜 This Day in History — November 2

  • 1889 — North and South Dakota were admitted to the Union on the same day. President Benjamin Harrison shuffled the papers before signing, so no one knows which came first. North Dakota is listed as the 39th state only because it's first alphabetically.
  • 1920 — Pittsburgh's KDKA broadcast the Harding–Cox election returns, the start of commercial radio. Warren Harding won the election on his 55th birthday.
  • 1947 — Howard Hughes flew the "Spruce Goose" (the H-4 Hercules) for its only flight: about a mile, roughly 70 feet over Long Beach Harbor.
  • Also: Quiz-show star Charles Van Doren admitted to Congress that Twenty-One was rigged (1959). The first crew moved into the International Space Station, which has been occupied without a break ever since (2000). Born today: Daniel Boone (1734), Marie Antoinette (1755), James K. Polk (1795) and Warren G. Harding (1865), the only two presidents who share a birthday, Burt Lancaster (1913), Stefanie Powers (1942), k.d. lang (1961), David Schwimmer (1966), Nelly (1974).

🧩 General Trivia

  • CivicsBy an 1845 law, Election Day is the Tuesday after the first Monday in November. That means it can never fall on Nov 1. This year it's Nov 3.
  • PresidentsJames K. Polk and Warren G. Harding were both born on Nov 2. They're the only presidents who share a birthday. 🟥 Harding was born in Blooming Grove, Ohio.
  • HorsesThe Melbourne Cup (first run in 1861) is a public holiday in Melbourne. The great horse Phar Lap is now in three museums: his heart in Canberra, his hide in Melbourne, and his skeleton in Wellington, New Zealand.
  • CultureOn Día de los Muertos, families decorate altars with orange cempasúchil (marigolds). People say the flowers' scent guides spirits home.
  • SpacePeople have lived aboard the ISS without a break since Nov 2, 2000. That's 26 years today.
  • TVCharles Van Doren's admission that Twenty-One was rigged inspired Robert Redford's film Quiz Show (1994).
  • MoviesGodzilla Minus One (2023) was the first Godzilla film to win an Oscar, for Visual Effects, on a budget under $15 million.
  • Food"Deviled" has meant spicy food since the 1700s. Today is National Deviled Egg Day.
  • MuppetsCookie Monster's birthday is listed as Nov 2. In the 1960s Jim Henson made an early version of him for a snack-food commercial.
