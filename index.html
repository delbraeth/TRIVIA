🧠 General Trivia

  • TVCBS wouldn't let I Love Lucy say "pregnant," so the scripts said "expecting." The 1953 episode where Little Ricky is born drew about 44 million viewers, more than Eisenhower's inauguration the next day.
  • TVLucy Ricardo's maiden name was McGillicuddy.
  • BaseballKirk Gibson's homer was his only plate appearance of the 1988 World Series. Vin Scully said: "In a year that has been so improbable, the impossible has happened."
  • BaseballThe Brewers' only World Series was 1982, a seven-game loss to the Cardinals. That team was nicknamed "Harvey's Wallbangers" after manager Harvey Kuenn.
  • BaseballOnly one MLB team has come back from 0–3 to win a postseason series: the 2004 Red Sox, against the Yankees.
  • History"Mata Hari" was a stage name, Malay for "eye of the day" (the sun). She was born Margaretha Zelle in the Netherlands.
  • ScienceOne of the largest living things on Earth is a honey fungus in Oregon's Malheur National Forest. It spreads underground over about 2,385 acres and may be thousands of years old.
  • FoodFresh cheese curds squeak because their stretchy protein strands rub against your teeth. They lose the squeak within a day or so, even in the fridge.
  • HistoryThe white cane for blind people is often traced to the Lions Club in Peoria, Illinois, in 1930. LBJ proclaimed the first White Cane Safety Day in 1964.
  • MusicBlessid Union of Souls, below, is a Cincinnati band.
