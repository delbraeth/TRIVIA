📜 This Day in History — October 28

  • 1886 — President Grover Cleveland dedicated the Statue of Liberty, 140 years ago today. That day, office workers threw ticker tape out of Wall Street windows, starting New York's first ticker-tape parade.
  • 1940 — Mussolini demanded that Greece let Italian troops in, and Prime Minister Ioannis Metaxas said "Óchi" ("No"). Greece still celebrates the date as Ohi Day.
  • 1965 — Workers placed the final section of St. Louis's Gateway Arch, at the top. It is 630 feet tall and 630 feet wide at the base.
  • Also: The Massachusetts colony voted to found the college that became Harvard (1636). Eli Whitney applied for a patent on the cotton gin (1793). Congress passed the Volstead Act over Wilson's veto (1919). John XXIII was elected pope (1958). Khrushchev agreed to remove Soviet missiles from Cuba (1962). Born today: Erasmus (1466, traditional date), Evelyn Waugh (1903), Francis Bacon the painter (1909), Jonas Salk (1914), Telma Hopkins (1948), Annie Potts (1952), Bill Gates (1955), Julia Roberts (1967), Joaquin Phoenix (1974).

🧩 General Trivia

  • HistoryBefore the Statue of Liberty was finished, its torch arm was displayed to raise money, first at the 1876 Centennial Exposition in Philadelphia and then in Madison Square Park.
  • FoodThe cacao tree's scientific name, Theobroma, is Greek for "food of the gods."
  • Language"Sí, se puede" started in Phoenix in 1972, during Cesar Chavez's fast. Barack Obama's 2008 campaign used it as "Yes, we can," and in 2012 he gave Huerta the Medal of Freedom.
  • SpaceBritain's satellite Prospero went up on Oct 28, 1971. It's the only British satellite launched on a British rocket (Black Arrow, from Woomera, Australia). The rocket program had already been canceled.
  • LawUnder the Volstead Act, any drink with more than 0.5% alcohol counted as "intoxicating."
  • VaticanThe pope's Swiss Guard dates to 1506. Recruits still have to be Catholic men from Switzerland.
  • MoviesJulia Roberts, born today in 1967, became the first actress paid $20 million for a film, for Erin Brockovich (2000). She won the Oscar for it.
  • ArchitectureThe Gateway Arch is the tallest man-made monument in the Western Hemisphere. Its top is reached by a tram of small pods inside each leg.
  • BaseballBen Williamson's hit was the Rays' first World Series walk-off since Brett Phillips's single in Game 4 in 2020. Tampa Bay lost both of its earlier World Series, in 2008 and 2020.
  • PoliticsIsrael's Arab parties are on track for 19 seats, almost double the 10 they hold now.
