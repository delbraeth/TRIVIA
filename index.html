📜 This Day in History — October 26

  • 1825 — The Erie Canal opened. Gov. DeWitt Clinton left Buffalo on the Seneca Chief and reached New York Harbor nine days later, where he poured a keg of Lake Erie water into the Atlantic, the "Wedding of the Waters." The canal ran 363 miles to Albany.
  • 1881 — The Gunfight at the O.K. Corral (145 years ago today) in Tombstone, Arizona Territory. Wyatt, Virgil and Morgan Earp and Doc Holliday faced the Clantons and McLaurys. It lasted about 30 seconds, and Billy Clanton and both McLaury brothers were killed.
  • 1977 — The last natural case of smallpox. Ali Maow Maalin, a hospital cook in Merca, Somalia, got sick and recovered. In 1980 the WHO declared smallpox eradicated, the only human disease ever wiped out.
  • Also: England's Football Association was founded in a London tavern (1863). Pan Am flew the first scheduled transatlantic jet service, on a Boeing 707 (1958). Austria declared permanent neutrality, now its National Day (1955). The Terminator opened (1984). Israel and Jordan signed a peace treaty (1994). Born today: Domenico Scarlatti (1685), Mahalia Jackson (1911), Jaclyn Smith (1945), Pat Sajak (1946, turns 80 today), Hillary Clinton (1947), Kevin Kline (1947), Dylan McDermott (1961), Cary Elwes (1962), Seth MacFarlane (1973).

🧩 General Trivia

  • HistoryThe O.K. Corral gunfight didn't happen in the corral. It was in a narrow vacant lot on Fremont Street, near C.S. Fly's photo studio, behind the corral. About 30 shots were fired in roughly 30 seconds.
  • AnimalsA mule has a donkey father and a horse mother. Flip the parents and you get a hinny. Both are almost always sterile.
  • GeographyTennessee borders 8 states, tied with Missouri for the most. Columbia, Tennessee, calls itself the "Mule Capital of the World" and holds Mule Day every spring.
  • TVBefore Wheel, Pat Sajak was a TV weatherman in Nashville (WSM) and then Los Angeles (KNBC). In Vietnam he was an Armed Forces Radio DJ who opened his show with "Good morning, Vietnam!"
  • FoodA pumpkin is a fruit, a type of berry called a pepo. Illinois grows more pumpkins than any other state, mostly for canning.
  • ScienceSmallpox is the only human disease ever eradicated. The only animal disease is rinderpest, declared gone in 2011.
  • MoviesO.J. Simpson was considered for the Terminator, and Schwarzenegger was first approached to play the hero, Kyle Reese. As the cyborg, Arnold has fewer than 20 lines.
  • F1If Antonelli clinches in Mexico, he'll be Italy's first champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953 and will beat Sebastian Vettel's record as youngest champion (23, in 2010).
  • BaseballMilwaukee's only other World Series was in 1982, as an American League team. "Harvey's Wallbangers" lost to the Cardinals in 7 games.
  • HistoryThe Erie Canal made New York City the country's top port, and it helped cement New York's nickname: the Empire State.
