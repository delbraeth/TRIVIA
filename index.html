📜 This Day in History — November 24

  • 1859 — Charles Darwin's On the Origin of Species was published in London by John Murray. All 1,250 copies of the first printing went to booksellers right away.
  • 1963 — Jack Ruby shot Lee Harvey Oswald in the basement of Dallas police headquarters. It was the first killing shown live on national TV.
  • 1971 — "D.B. Cooper" hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305 out of Portland. He parachuted out with $200,000 and was never found.
  • 1974 — In Hadar, Ethiopia, Donald Johanson and Tom Gray found "Lucy," a 3.2-million-year-old human ancestor. The team named her after the Beatles' "Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds."
  • Born today: Baruch Spinoza (1632), Laurence Sterne (1713), Zachary Taylor (1784), Carlo Collodi (1826), Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec (1864), Scott Joplin (traditional date, 1868), Dale Carnegie (1888), William F. Buckley Jr. (1925), Katherine Heigl (1978). Died today: Freddie Mercury (1991).

🧩 General Trivia

  • Science🧬 Darwin didn't coin "survival of the fittest." Herbert Spencer did, in 1864. Darwin added it to the fifth edition of Origin in 1869.
  • Mystery🪂 In 1980, 8-year-old Brian Ingram found $5,800 of D.B. Cooper's ransom on a Columbia River beach. The serial numbers matched. The FBI stopped actively investigating the case in 2016.
  • Science🦴 "Lucy" is about 40% of a skeleton. She stood about 3.5 feet tall. In Ethiopia she's called Dinkinesh, which means "you are marvelous."
  • PresidentsZachary Taylor, "Old Rough and Ready," never voted in a presidential election before his own in 1848. He died 16 months into his term.
  • Food🐟 "Sardine" isn't one species. It covers several small, oily fish. The name probably comes from the Italian island of Sardinia.
  • Food🦃 Green bean casserole was invented in 1955 by Dorcas Reilly in Campbell's test kitchen. Her original recipe card is in the National Inventors Hall of Fame.
  • Movies🎹 Scott Joplin's "The Entertainer" became a hit again thanks to The Sting (1973). Marvin Hamlisch's adaptation of Joplin's music won an Oscar.
  • Music🎤 "Bohemian Rhapsody" hit No. 2 in the U.S. in 1992, 17 years after it came out. The headbanging scene in Wayne's World brought it back.
  • Olympics🏊 At the 1956 Melbourne Games, Australia swept the men's 100m freestyle: Jon Henricks, John Devitt and Gary Chapman.
  • Football🦃 The Chiefs and Bills are 2nd and 3rd in yards per game, and they play each other Thanksgiving night.
