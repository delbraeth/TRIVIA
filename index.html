🌍 World Capitals

🇪🇸 Spain — today is its Fiesta Nacional (Día de la Hispanidad). Capital?
Madrid. It's also where the Bengals play the Falcons on Nov 8, at the Bernabéu.

🇩🇴 Dominican Republic — the oldest European city still standing in the Americas. Capital?
Santo Domingo, founded in 1496 by Bartholomew Columbus, Christopher's brother.

🇸🇻 El Salvador — its name means "The Savior." Capital?
San Salvador. Don't mix it up with San Salvador Island in the Bahamas, Columbus's first landfall.

🇰🇲 Comoros — an island nation between Mozambique and Madagascar. Capital?
Moroni, on Grande Comore (Ngazidja).

🧠 General Trivia

  • HistoryColumbus sailed with the Niña, Pinta and Santa María. The Santa María ran aground off Haiti on Christmas Day 1492, and its timbers were used to build the fort La Navidad.
  • HolidayThis is the first year since 2020 that Columbus Day / Indigenous Peoples' Day falls on Oct 12 itself. It became a federal holiday in 1937 and moved to a Monday in 1971.
  • FoodGumbo (National Gumbo Day) gets its name from a West African word for okra, ki ngombo.
  • FoodA pork "Boston butt" (National Pulled Pork Day) comes from the shoulder, not the rear. Colonial Boston butchers packed it in barrels called butts.
  • GeographyMontpelier, Vermont (National Vermont Day) is the smallest state capital by population, and the only one without a McDonald's.
  • DrinksThe Hurricane cocktail comes from Pat O'Brien's in New Orleans in the 1940s. It's named for the hurricane-lamp-shaped glass.
  • SportsThe Texas–Oklahoma Red River Rivalry is played at the Cotton Bowl during the State Fair of Texas. The winner takes home the Golden Hat trophy.
  • ScienceThe sperm whale has the largest brain of any animal, about 17 pounds.
  • GeographyBecause the Isthmus of Panama curves, some spots there let you watch the sun rise over the Pacific.
  • Pop CultureStreet Fighter II (1991)'s Chun-Li is widely credited as the first playable woman in a one-on-one fighting game. The Street Fighter movie opens Friday.
  • WorldJapan's Sports Day is today (the second Monday in October). It marks the opening of the 1964 Tokyo Olympics.
