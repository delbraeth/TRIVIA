📜 This Day in History — October 29

  • 1923 — Turkey became a republic, with Mustafa Kemal (later Atatürk) as its first president. Ankara had been made the capital two weeks earlier. Today is Republic Day there.
  • 1929 — "Black Tuesday." More than 16 million shares traded on the New York Stock Exchange, a record that stood for almost 40 years. The crash helped bring on the Great Depression.
  • 1998 — Sen. John Glenn went back to space on the shuttle Discovery at age 77, 36 years after he became the first American to orbit Earth. He was the oldest person in space until William Shatner flew at 90 in 2021.
  • Also: Sir Walter Raleigh was executed in London (1618). Israel invaded Egypt's Sinai, starting the Suez Crisis (1956). UCLA sent the first message over ARPANET (1969). Superstorm Sandy made landfall in New Jersey (2012). Born today: James Boswell (1740), Richard Dreyfuss (1947), Kate Jackson (1948), Dan Castellaneta (1957), Winona Ryder (1971), Gabrielle Union and Tracee Ellis Ross (1972).

🏭 Founded & Invented

On Oct 29, 1969, UCLA sent the first message over ARPANET to the Stanford Research Institute. They were trying to type "LOGIN." What actually arrived before the system crashed?
"LO" (sent by student Charley Kline in Leonard Kleinrock's lab)

Candy corn, once marketed as "Chicken Feed," was created in the 1880s by George Renninger of which Philadelphia company?

The Ouija board was patented in 1891 and first sold by which Baltimore company?

In 1928, a former dairy worker for Milton Hershey started selling peanut butter cups under his own name. What was his name?
H. B. Reese

Leo Hirshfield's chewy chocolate candy, first made in New York in 1896, was named for his daughter Clara's nickname. What is the candy?

🟠 Local: Ludwig Hudepohl founded a Cincinnati brewery in 1885 that lasted for more than a century. Locals called its beer what?

🧩 General Trivia

  • MoviesMichael Myers's mask in Halloween (1978) was a $1.98 Captain Kirk mask. The crew painted it white and widened the eyeholes.
  • HistoryHarry Houdini died on Halloween in 1926, in Detroit, of peritonitis from a ruptured appendix.
  • FolkloreThe first jack-o'-lanterns in Ireland and Scotland were carved from turnips. Immigrants to America switched to pumpkins.
  • GeographyRomania's Bran Castle is sold to tourists as "Dracula's Castle," but Bram Stoker never went to Romania.
  • CandySnickers (1930) is named after the Mars family's favorite horse.
  • Music"Monster Mash" by Bobby "Boris" Pickett reached No. 1 just before Halloween 1962, and it charted again in 1973.
  • AnimalsVampire bats can starve after about two nights without a meal, so well-fed bats regurgitate blood to share with hungry roostmates.
  • PlacesAnoka, Minnesota calls itself the "Halloween Capital of the World." It began a citywide celebration in 1920 to keep kids from pulling pranks.
  • WordsThe phrase "trick or treat" first appeared in print in 1927, in an Alberta, Canada newspaper.
  • BaseballWith the Brewers' win, only four teams have never won a World Series: the Mariners, Padres, Rays and Rockies.
