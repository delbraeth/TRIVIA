📜 This Day in History — November 19

  • 1493 — On his second voyage, Columbus landed on Puerto Rico. He named it San Juan Bautista. Puerto Rico marks today as Discovery Day.
  • 1863 — Lincoln gave the Gettysburg Address at the dedication of the Soldiers' National Cemetery. It was about 272 words and took about two minutes. The main speaker, Edward Everett, had just spoken for about two hours.
  • 1969 — Apollo 12's Pete Conrad and Alan Bean landed in the Ocean of Storms, close to the Surveyor 3 probe. That same day, Pelé scored his 1,000th goal, a penalty kick against Vasco da Gama at the Maracanã.
  • 1977 — Anwar Sadat became the first Arab leader to make an official visit to Israel. He addressed the Knesset the next day.

🌍 Capitals

🇵🇷 Columbus reached it on Nov 19, 1493. What's the capital of Puerto Rico?
San Juan, founded in 1521. Its original name was Puerto Rico ("rich port"). The names later swapped.

🇱🇰 Colombo is Sri Lanka's biggest city. What's the official (legislative) capital?
Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, a suburb of Colombo

🇨🇦 What's the capital of Nunavut, Canada's newest territory (1999)?
Iqaluit. It was called Frobisher Bay until 1987.

🇹🇿 Not Dar es Salaam: what's the official capital of Tanzania?
Dodoma

🧩 General Trivia

  • SpaceA light-day is about 16 billion miles (25.9 billion km). Voyager 1 just became the first spacecraft that far from Earth, after 49 years of flight.
  • Pop CultureHaymitch Abernathy won the 50th Hunger Games, a "Quarter Quell" that had 48 tributes instead of the usual 24.
  • GamesThe Monopoly man was called "Rich Uncle Pennybags" until 1999, when he was renamed "Mr. Monopoly." He has never worn a monocle, though many people remember one.
  • History🦃 Sarah Josepha Hale, who wrote "Mary Had a Little Lamb," lobbied for 17 years for a national Thanksgiving. Lincoln proclaimed one in 1863.
  • Sports🏀 The Cincinnati Royals' Oscar Robertson averaged a triple-double for a whole season (1961–62). Nobody did it again until Russell Westbrook in 2016–17.
  • TechBluetooth is named for 10th-century Danish king Harald "Bluetooth" Gormsson. Its logo combines his initials in Norse runes.
  • FoodThe kiwi fruit was once known as the "Chinese gooseberry." New Zealand exporters renamed it after the kiwi bird in 1959.
  • SciencePlants make caffeine as a natural pesticide. It can paralyze or kill insects that eat the leaves.
  • GeographyPuerto Ricans have been U.S. citizens since 1917 (the Jones Act). People living on the island still can't vote in the general election for president.
  • Oddity🚽 World Toilet Day was started by Singapore's World Toilet Organization (founded 2001). The UN made it official in 2013.
