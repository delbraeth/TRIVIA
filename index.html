📜 This Day in History — November 13

  • 1927 — The Holland Tunnel opened under the Hudson between New York and New Jersey. It was the first mechanically ventilated underwater tunnel for cars.
  • 1940 — Disney's Fantasia premiered at the Broadway Theatre in New York, in "Fantasound," an early stereo system.
  • 1956 — In Browder v. Gayle, the U.S. Supreme Court affirmed that segregated buses in Montgomery were unconstitutional. The Montgomery bus boycott ended about five weeks later.
  • 1982 — The Vietnam Veterans Memorial was dedicated in Washington. Its black granite wall now lists more than 58,000 names.
  • 1985 — The Nevado del Ruiz volcano erupted in Colombia, and mudflows buried the town of Armero. About 23,000 people died.
  • Born today: St. Augustine (354), Edwin Booth (1833), Robert Louis Stevenson (1850), Louis Brandeis (1856), Garry Marshall (1934), Jean Seberg (1938), Whoopi Goldberg (1955), Jimmy Kimmel (1967), Steve Zahn (1967), Gerard Butler (1969).

🧩 General Trivia

  • CalendarToday is 2026's third Friday the 13th, after February and March. Three is the most a year can have. The next one is Aug 13, 2027.
  • WordsFear of Friday the 13th is called paraskevidekatriaphobia, or friggatriskaidekaphobia. Fear of the number 13 by itself is triskaidekaphobia.
  • MoviesIn the original Friday the 13th (1980), the killer is Jason's mother, Mrs. Voorhees (Betsy Palmer). Jason doesn't wear the hockey mask until Part III (1982).
  • BaseballCrow-Armstrong is only the seventh player in the 40–40 club (40 HR and 40 steals in a season).
  • BaseballÁlvarez is the third Astros MVP, after Jeff Bagwell (1994) and José Altuve (2017). He played only 48 games in 2025 and 158 this year.
  • FoodIndian pudding is a New England dessert of cornmeal, which colonists called "Indian meal," baked for hours with milk and molasses.
  • BusinessColette Kress has been Nvidia's CFO since 2013. In that time, yearly revenue went from about $4.1 billion to $215.9 billion.
  • HockeySebastian Aho, who scored twice in Helsinki, was born in Rauma, Finland. A different Sebastian Aho, a Swedish defenseman, also plays in the NHL.
  • GolfMiles Russell, a high school senior, shot 67 in the first round in Austin.
  • GeographyNevado del Ruiz is about 17,450 feet tall. The 1985 disaster is the deadliest eruption since Mount Pelée in 1902.
